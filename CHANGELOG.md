# Holy Math

## 2.1.0

**Holy Paladins are here.** Log in on a Holy Paladin and Holy Math works the same way it does for priests: your HPS and stat values, item and spell tooltips, the Consumables tab and the Math tab, all with paladin spells and talents.

- **Your fight** (Stats tab) has two paladin settings: the share of your heals on your Oath of Light target, and how many people Light's Vigil reaches. Also `/hm set oath <percent>` and `/hm set vigil <1-5>`.
- **Best for you** tries Light's Vigil, Holy Shock, Holy Light on Infusion of Light procs, and Flash of Light or Holy Light as your main heal.
- **Item tooltips** know paladin gear: mail, plate, shields, paladin weapons, and the Justice Armor set.
- The numbers follow wowsims' paladin code and DoubleZug's paladin spreadsheet. Players checked crits with Holy Power (175%) and Illumination on Light's Vigil (mana back once per Vigil), and a level 42 Holy Paladin's spells, talents and tooltips matched in game. Still unchecked: how much of Oath of Light's bonus a low rank gets.

**Also new**

- The Settings tab is now called **Stats**.
- A new icon: a page of figures, for both classes.
